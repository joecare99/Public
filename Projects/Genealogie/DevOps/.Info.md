# Genealogie workspace planning

## Scope

Track the systematic reconstruction of AhnWin's decompiled Delphi sources and
the migration of its Lazarus build path. Keep original binaries, databases, and
reference resources unchanged. Put implementation evidence and reusable
reconstruction knowledge in the separate wiki at `C:\Projekte\Delphi\docs`.

## Working agreements

- Break work into dependency-ordered backlog items and complete one validated
  slice at a time.
- Record status, acceptance criteria, risks, and evidence in each feature/task.
- Do not use personal genealogy records as examples or copy them into the wiki.
- Keep unresolved decompiler symbols and behavior visible; do not replace
  unknown behavior with empty stubs.
- The accepted reporting target is Lazarus LazReport. QuickReport layouts and
  workflows must be migrated with behavior evidence, not hidden by a fake
  compatibility type.
- A temporary QuickReport compile-compatibility layer is approved only when
  every unsupported operation fails explicitly; it does not satisfy or replace
  the LazReport migration.
